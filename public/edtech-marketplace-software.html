EdTech Marketplace Software Platform

Families searching for tutors, educators, and childcare providers still depend on informal networks. Velttora builds marketplace software that connects supply and demand with verification, scheduling, payments, and learning outcomes in one platform.

Eudify is our education marketplace: science-based learning plans, provider onboarding, trust and safety controls, and operational tooling for two-sided growth.

Why generic marketplaces fail in education

Education is not a standard gig economy vertical. Parents need verified credentials, pedagogical fit, and progress visibility — not just star ratings. Providers need recurring bookings, compliant payouts, and reputation they can carry across cities.

Purpose-built EdTech software encodes those requirements: KYC, curriculum milestones, session logs, and quality signals that generic classifieds or social groups cannot offer.

Platform modules

  • Family and provider profiles with structured onboarding
  • Matching based on location, skills, availability, and child needs
  • Booking, calendar, and Stripe Connect payment flows
  • Curriculum and milestone tracking per learner
  • Moderation, incident reporting, and trust & safety workflows
  • Operator analytics for retention, GMV, and provider quality
