SportsTech Platform for Fitness Competitions

Functional fitness events still run on spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and manual heat assignments. Velttora builds competition software that handles registration, payments, heat planning, scoring, and live leaderboards from one operating system.

Hycap is our SportsTech platform for CrossFit, Hyrox, Iron Race, and hybrid formats — built for organizers who need professional event ops without enterprise complexity.

Operational pain without dedicated software

Organizers lose hours balancing heats by skill level. Athletes wait for results. Spectators refresh Instagram instead of a trusted leaderboard. Sponsors see amateur ops. Scaling to multiple cities becomes impossible when every event is rebuilt from scratch.

End-to-end event workflow

  • Athlete profiles with performance history and qualification data
  • Online registration and integrated checkout
  • Automated heat assignment by skill and category
  • Judge tools and real-time score entry
  • Live leaderboard and public results pages
  • Post-event analytics for organizers and communities

LATAM-first, global-ready

Velttora targets Latin America's fast-growing functional fitness community while designing APIs and data models that scale internationally. Mobile-first UX, real-time updates, and low marginal cost per additional event are core design goals.

Who should adopt this platform

  • Independent competition organizers and box owners
  • Regional fitness leagues and federations
  • SportsTech startups productizing event operations
  • Sponsors and media partners needing reliable live data
