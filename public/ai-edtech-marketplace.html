EdTech Marketplace Software Platform

Velttora develops marketplace software for education and childcare ecosystems. The platform combines trust & safety controls, provider verification, smart matching, and progress tracking in one operating layer.

What this platform includes

  • Smart matching between families and verified education providers.
  • Provider onboarding, verification, and reputation workflows.
  • Curriculum and learning-plan tracking across child milestones.
  • Scheduling, booking, and payment orchestration for recurring services.
  • Analytics for retention, quality, and marketplace growth.

Who this is for

EdTech operators, childcare networks, and education startups building trusted two-sided marketplaces.
