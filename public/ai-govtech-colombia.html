GovTech Platform

GovTech Software Platform for Cities in Colombia

Velttora develops GovTech software for municipalities that need faster citizen request handling, better service-level visibility, and less manual triage. Our platform uses automation and data models to classify reports, detect duplicates, and route each request to the right department.

Tambien trabajamos en espanol: desarrollamos software GovTech para equipos publicos en LATAM con implementacion y soporte bilingue.

What city teams can do with this platform

  • Receive citizen reports through mobile-first flows in under 60 seconds.
  • Auto-classify issues (roads, lighting, waste, safety, water) with automation.
  • Detect duplicates and prioritize by impact and urgency.
  • Track SLA and response performance by zone, team, and category.
  • Export data for audits and public transparency initiatives.

Who this is for

City innovation offices, mayors, public service secretariats, and regional government teams in Colombia and LATAM modernizing case management and public issue workflows.
